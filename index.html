02What AI Changes

AI changes what the company is.

Cloud, SaaS, mobile, CRM changed how work was done. AI is a different kind of shift.

Organisations were built around knowledge. Who has it, who produces it, who synthesises it. AI has made knowledge abundant.

The companies that win the next decade will be built on judgment, learning velocity, and the orchestration of humans and AI working together.

"I started Human × AI after sitting in a room of smart operators whose AI strategy was to automate, drive efficiency, take cost out. That was the whole strategy. They had not lived through the prior transitions. Most rooms are still that room. The point of this playbook is to change that."
— Karine Allouche, Founder

04The Methodology

Four moves, run in order.

The work that produces results is always the same work, in the same sequence. The discipline is the methodology, not the technology.

01

Capabilities.

What outcomes the function exists to deliver, defined before any workflow is touched. Most redesigns fail here, by skipping straight to tools. The capability question comes first because everything downstream is in service of it.

02

Workflows.

Current state mapped honestly. Then redesigned end-to-end. Each step classified as autonomous, augmented, or human-essential. The classification is the work, and it is the work that automation alone cannot do.

03

Judgment.

The deliberate placement of human decision-making, where it creates more value than system autonomy would. Judgment is the most expensive resource in the company of tomorrow. It belongs where it is decisive.

04

Data flow & learning loops.

Data as shared infrastructure. Every correction logged. The organisation gets smarter when its people work, or it leaks value. The learning loop is what compounds; without it, the redesign is a snapshot, not a trajectory.
