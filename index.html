01The Position

Not a thesis about AI. A position drawn from inside the work.

The Human × AI playbook is drawn from leading the Human × AI community of 1,200+ senior leaders on Substack, and from inside the work itself. Running the AI redesign at Chegg today, while advising tech and tech-services companies through theirs. Four prior tech transitions before that: leading the largest corporate and governmental AI programs at Coursera; licensing to SaaS at Microsoft and Oracle; web to mobile at Microsoft and Oracle; on-prem to cloud at NetApp.

The pattern held every time. The companies that won did not buy better technology. They redesigned how their people and processes worked around what the technology now made possible. The ones that added it as a layer produced incremental gains and structural fragility.

02What AI Changes

AI changes what the company is.

Cloud, SaaS, mobile, CRM changed how work was done. AI is a different kind of shift.

Organisations were built around knowledge. Who has it, who produces it, who synthesises it. AI has made knowledge abundant.

The companies that win the next decade will be built on judgment, learning velocity, and the orchestration of humans and AI working together.

"I started Human × AI after sitting in a room of smart operators whose AI strategy was to automate, drive efficiency, take cost out. That was the whole strategy. They had not lived through the prior transitions. Most rooms are still that room. The point of this playbook is to change that."
— Karine Allouche, Founder
03AI-Native Leadership

The redesign creates the conditions. The leader holds the lead.

AI makes intelligence, knowledge, and speed abundant. What stays scarce becomes the source of value: judgment, purpose, integrity, courage, empathy, groundedness.

The new conscious leader, in integrity, grounded, holding the truth for the path forward, is the highest-leverage asset in the company of tomorrow.

04The Methodology

Four moves, run in order.

The work that produces results is always the same work, in the same sequence. The discipline is the methodology, not the technology.

01

Capabilities.

What outcomes the function exists to deliver. Defined before any workflow is touched.

02

Workflows.

Mapped honestly, redesigned end-to-end. Each step classified autonomous, augmented, or human-essential.

03

Judgment.

The deliberate placement of human decision-making, where it changes the outcome.

04

Data flow & loops.

Data as shared infrastructure. Every correction logged. The function compounds.

05The Pattern

The results.

Across the redesigns in my track record at Chegg and current advisory work in tech and tech-services, the pattern is consistent. The ranges below are directional, drawn from operator-grade engagements, not industry benchmarks.

Cost reduction
10–25%
P&L cost out, sustained across the redesigned function.
FTE reduction
15–30%
Classified, not cut. Headcount may move; leverage is the target.
Innovation delivery
5–10×
From months to weeks. Cycle time compresses as workflows redesign.
Customer validation
Months → Weeks
Pulled forward in the cycle. Decisions earlier; risk lower.

Margin expansion alongside value creation. Cost-out and growth in the same motion, not as a tradeoff. Scalability of key human capital. The most valuable people scale further; their judgment reaches more of the work, because the work is built to receive it.

The numbers will vary. The pattern doesn't. Companies that run the Human × AI playbook don't deploy a tool. They redesign the work, and everything that follows changes with it.
